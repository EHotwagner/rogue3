## §2 What worked

- **The render-and-look loop earned its place twice, and the second time it was someone else's eyes.** Looking at my own first frames caught two legibility defects that 224 green tests could not: the enemy telegraph's arrowhead was drawn from the tip back *along* the bar, so both arms fell inside the bar's own 10-unit stroke and rendered as a single perpendicular tick; and the dodge trail was 26 units at alpha 150, a smudge the size of the player's own facing pip. Then an independent visual critic found four more of the same class — an invisible bomb pickup, three near-identical yellows for coin/coin-3/key, every shop consumable drawn in the player's own colour, and the `ACTIVE n/m` label drawn straight through the dial it names. **No requirements-derived assertion in this product can see any of these**, which is precisely the argument the milestone was filed on.
- **Mutation testing is the only honest way to claim a test guards something.** The code critic reverted each fix in turn and re-ran the suite. Sixteen of eighteen mutations went red. The two that stayed green were real gaps: restoring the exact pre-M13 shop and reward screen coordinates left the whole suite passing, so **the milestone's third row had no regression guard at all**, and freezing the new cost counter was invisible to `dotnet test`. Both are now guarded. I would not have found either by reading my own tests.
- **One geometry, two consumers, held up.** Moving the wall band into `Model.roomWallSlabs` and having both `Render.roomWallsScene` and the player sweep consume it made the collider hole *findable*: the critic could derive the drawn geometry from the renderer and the collider from the model and compare them slab for slab. A collider maintained separately from the picture would have hidden the hidden-wall defect indefinitely.
- **Exact-equality cost drivers caught an arithmetic consequence I had not predicted.** Excluding hidden walls from the collider gap changed the maximum-content room from eight wall slabs to seven, and `collision.shot-wall-queries` moved 832 → 820 — exactly `736 + 6 × 7 × 2`. A ceiling-style budget would have absorbed that silently; the equality check made the change state its own arithmetic.
- **Declaring a touch-set extension on the item before implementing was cheap and removed all ambiguity.** The item's `Paths:` could not satisfy its own rows, the check against the only other in-flight item took one command, and the PR reviewer can see the reasoning at the time rather than reconstructed after.

#### §4.1 The audit-binding gate has no green path once any audit cites the excuse ledger

Over a copy of `feedback/` and `scripts/` at `562e905`, four consecutive `--grandfather` runs produced four different ledger digests (`029bedeb…`, `cc51d61a…`, `f977ecdb…`, `e967ef49…`) and the check still exited 1, with the checker printing the loop itself: `bound 76fa77c3f6b5 now e967ef49a0be -- file changed again since it was excused (ledger excused f977ecdb462a, file is now e967ef49a0be)`.
