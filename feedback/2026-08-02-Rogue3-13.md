## §2 What worked

**Characterization assertions that name their issue.** Board item #20 left two tests asserting the *gap* — `Expect.isEmpty bought.PlayerItems "EHotwagner/rogue3#47: the one wired shop reducer grants no item"` — in `M3CombatHealthCurrencyTests.fs` and `M10AcceptanceDeterminismTests.fs`. The first run after the fix failed exactly those two and printed the issue number. The fix announced itself instead of passing silently, and I did not have to hunt for which acceptance scenarios covered the area. This is cheap and should be the standing convention for any knowingly-deferred defect.

**M13's pickup machinery generalized without modification.** `Model.collectFloorPickups` and `Model.placeRoomFixtures` already solved "a reward the player can walk onto, at the position it is drawn, removed durably in the step it is applied". The room reward is the same shape one fixture along, so `collectRoomReward` is a near-transcription and reuses `placeRoomFixtures` as the single position source that `Render` already consumes.

**`fsgg-coord say` / `inbox` as a real negotiation channel.** The `widen` that collided returned exit 6 and auto-notified both live workers; both replied with substantive, decision-changing content within the cycle (§4.5). Coordination here was not ceremonial.

## §3 What did not

**The first draft of the change introduced a scoring regression.** Routing a shop `Consumable` purchase through `Model.applyFloorPickup` is correct for the effect and wrong for the accounting: that function credits `RunStats.CoinsCollected`, and `runScore` pays `CoinsCollected * 5`. The shop's pool-exhausted fallback offer is a `Coin3` priced at 3, so a purchase that nets zero coins minted 15 run score, repeatably. Caught by a fresh-context critic, not by the suite (§4.3).

**The first draft's own regression sweep was blind to the case that dominates a real run.** It enumerated floors with `FloorGeneration.generate`, which hands every floor a fresh item pool; production carries `model.M5ItemPool` across floors through `generateWithPool`. On the boot seed the difference is 12 item rewards and 0 fallbacks versus 4 and 8 (§4.4).

**The first draft passed a green suite with 26 of 57 mutations surviving.** The fix itself could not be reverted silently — every mutation on `grantItem`, the `loadM5Room` line and the fixed-step call site died — but the surface introduced *around* it was largely unpinned (§4.2).

**A near-miss on name shadowing.** `Model.fs` opens `Rogue3.Geometry` and runs 2,500+ lines; a two-line helper named `add` would have shadowed `Vec2.add` for every later definition in the file. Caught by inspection before the first build, not by any tool (§4.7).
