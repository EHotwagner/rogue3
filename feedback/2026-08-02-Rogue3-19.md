#### §4.7 A digest pinned over a run output cannot be settled by the excuse ledger

- **Kind:** defect
- **Impact:** Fourteen `file:` bindings across twelve merged audits pin a sha256 over an artifact the merge gate rewrites (sixteen raw citations; the checker counts one binding per audit and locator, so three duplicates in one audit collapse to one). While the artifacts were tracked, workers kept those bindings alive by reverting the files by hand after every gate run. The moment the artifacts stop being tracked the bindings become unsatisfiable in a way the documented remedy cannot reach.
- **Expected:** `--grandfather` settles any stale binding in one pass and keeps it settled; the module docstring says so and proves it for the audit-to-audit case.

Fresh in the very checkout where CI was about to read `<missing>`. An entry written from a developer's tree therefore fails in CI and one written from CI fails on the developer's tree. This is `rogue3#38`'s shape arriving through a different door.
- **Evidence:** command:python3 scripts/check-audit-bindings.py; file:scripts/check-audit-bindings.py; issue:EHotwagner/rogue3#38

- **Recurrence:** new as a mechanism finding. Adjacent to `issue:EHotwagner/rogue3#40`, `issue:EHotwagner/rogue3#49` and `issue:EHotwagner/rogue3#38`.
