# M13 Room Transition Pickups And World Space State Clarifications

## Source Specification
- work/014-m13-room-transition-pickups-world-state/spec.md

## Clarification Questions
- CQ-001 [AMB:AMB-001]: How much of the departed room does the slide draw — the whole room including its obstacles, pickups and fixtures, or its shell?
- CQ-002 [AMB:AMB-002]: Does the wall collider fill a doorway whose door is locked, hidden or combat-sealed?
- CQ-003 [AMB:AMB-003]: Does "walk onto it and collect it" apply to the room-clear drop and the shop stock as well as to obstacle drops?
- CQ-004 [AMB:AMB-004]: Is the floor banner a required cost driver in a steady-state workload, or a non-performance one?
- CQ-005: Which authored digests move, and how is a moved digest re-declared without laundering an unreviewed change?
- CQ-006: Pickup collection and the wall collider both land on the fixed-step hot path. How is their cost made observable?
- CQ-007: Where do the M13 frames live, given `M11PlayabilityLegibilityTests` asserts the M11 frame directory contains exactly sixteen named subdirectories?
- CQ-008: What exactly does "the drawn wall and the collider are one value" mean in code, given `Render` compiles after `Model`?

## Answers
- CA-001: The departed room's actors are provably gone, and its furniture is not recoverable. `Model.playerRoomIntentsIn` refuses a crossing through a doorway the derived combat lock has sealed (`doorwaySealed`), so the only rooms a player can leave are rooms with no live enemies; and `loadM5Room` sets `EnemyBullets=[]` and `ShotSpawns=[]` on entry, so the departed room's projectiles are already discarded today. What is *not* recoverable is the departed room's obstacle list, drop, reward and shop stock: those live only in the room-local model fields, which `loadM5Room` has just overwritten with the entered room's. Re-deriving them per frame from `Floor.Rooms.[id].Interior` would put a second, renderer-owned generation of room content beside the model's — the exact pattern `EHotwagner/rogue3#20` is already open against in this product. What actually fills the screen during a crossing is the floor, the 24-unit stone band and the door panels: at 1280x720 the wall band and its trim are the only thing at the edges of the frame, and the M11 frames show a room reads as a room from its shell alone.
- CA-002: It cannot, without making the key door unreachable. `doorwaySensorDepth = 14.0` and `wallThickness = 24.0`, and `playerRoomIntentsIn` raises `UnlockDoor` only while `doorwaySensorContains` is true — depth in `[0, 14]`. A collider spanning a locked doorway would stop the player's centre at depth `24 + playerRadius = 37`, so the sensor could never fire and M11's `FR-002 key door spends exactly one key` route, which is driven through movement keys alone, would become unreachable. The roadmap row's own words are "the player cannot stand inside the wall band **the renderer draws**", and the band the renderer draws is `Render.roomWallsScene`'s slabs, which already open a `2 * doorwayHalfSpan` gap at every wall that has a door of any state. Making those slabs the collider closes the row exactly and leaves every doorway an affordance.
- CA-003: Only obstacle drops. The roadmap row names them, and the other two are different mechanics with different owners. The room-clear drop is `Rogue3.Entities.CombatRoom.Drop : PickupKind option` — a field on a record in `src/Rogue3/Entities.fs`, which this work item does not touch and which carries no position to give it; making it collectable means changing that record, which is a separate change with its own blast radius. Shop stock is not walked onto at all: it is bought with `InteractM5Shop`, which already debits coins or a key. Extending collection to either would be scope this item cannot discharge honestly.
- CA-004: Non-performance. `FloorNameTicks` is 0 in the initial model of every one of the seven workloads — the banner is a timed announcement set to 240 by `DescendFloor` and decremented every step — so no steady-state workload frame holds it, and a workload manufactured to hold it would measure one text node. The other four HUD regions render in every frame and simply inherit the disposition `ui.hud-score` already had, `RequiredIn [ "firing"; "maximum-content" ]`, one each.
- CA-005: All seven, and both UI-route source digests. `PerformanceEvidence.definitionDigest` folds in `modelDefinitionFingerprint`, which is `Determinism.encode (workload.InitialState())` — and the encoder is fully reflective over the record graph, so changing `M6CameraTransition`'s field set and `M5ObstacleDrops`'s element type moves the encoding of every model, including `initialModel`. It also folds in `intentPolicy` (which carries `MaximumExpectedScale` and the structural budgets) and `executableSource` (the workload's own marker-fenced source text). Re-declaring is not laundering as long as the *reason* each digest moved is stated and the evidence run is the source of the new value: the emitted `definitionDigest` is copied from the run that also proves p95, p99, scene nodes, catch-up and every exact cost-driver equality still hold. A digest that moves for a reason not on that list is a signal to investigate.
- CA-006: With counters, not with prose. Pickup collection adds a per-step scan of the floor-pickup list against the player circle; the wall collider adds slabs to the player's swept cast. Both get deterministic delta counters on the model — a new `TotalFloorPickupCandidates`, and the existing `TotalWallQueries`, whose `MaximumExpected` moves from 736 by exactly `6 * <wall slabs> * <steps>` in the same accounting the obstacle sweep already uses. Both are bound to `maximum-content`, where `costDriverProblems` demands **exact** equality rather than an upper bound, so an unintended extra query reds the evidence run instead of hiding under a ceiling.
- CA-007: In their own directory, `readiness/014-m13-room-transition-pickups-world-state/frames/`, produced by their own harness. `M11PlayabilityLegibilityTests` line 362-366 asserts the M11 frame directory's subdirectory set is *exactly* the sixteen authored names, so an M13 frame written there would red M11. The M13 harness is a sibling of the M11 one and follows its shape: `#r` the built assemblies, render `View.view` through `Render.toPng`, print each frame's element histogram. Its home is `scripts/render-m13-frames.fsx` — a new file, so it can collide with nothing.
- CA-008: One exported function, `Model.roomWallSlabs`, returning the four walls with a gap at every wall that carries a door. `Render.roomWallsScene` maps it to filled rectangles and adds the trim stroke; `Model.stepInput` passes the same list to `Collision.sweepCircle` as additional static rects. The geometry constants it needs — `wallThickness` in particular — move from `Render` to `Model` because `Model.fs` compiles first; `Render.wallThickness` stays as a re-export so `M11PlayabilityLegibilityTests`, which reads `Render.wallThickness` and `Render.doorApron`, is unaffected. There is then no second description to drift: deleting a slab removes it from the picture and from the collider in the same edit.

## Decisions
- DEC-001 [AMB:AMB-001] [CQ-001] [CA-001]: The slide draws the departed room's **shell**: its floor background, its wall band, its door presentations taken from the floor graph with the combat lock lifted (a room you can walk out of is cleared), and its trapdoor fixture when the floor records one. It is emitted as one new gameplay-visual element, `DepartedRoom`, on the floor-background layer, with its scene pre-translated one full playfield along the axis the crossing came from — so the single existing camera translate in `Render.viewIn` moves both rooms together and there is still exactly one camera transform in the product. The departed room's obstacles, drop, reward and shop stock are not drawn; this is recorded as a known limit rather than hidden, and it is what DEC-009 defers.
- DEC-002 [AMB:AMB-002] [CQ-002] [CA-002]: The collider is exactly `Model.roomWallSlabs` — the wall band with a `2 * doorwayHalfSpan` gap at every wall that carries a door of any state. Door panels are not colliders. A player may therefore still stand in the mouth of a locked, hidden or sealed doorway; that is the affordance `UnlockDoor` needs, and it is stated here rather than left to be discovered as a gap in the row.
- DEC-003 [AMB:AMB-003] [CQ-003] [CA-003]: Collection applies to obstacle drops only. `Model.M5ObstacleDrops` becomes `FloorPickup list` where `FloorPickup = { Id: int; Kind: Rogue3.Entities.PickupKind; Position: Vec2 }`, the id and position are the destroyed obstacle's, and the positionless representation is removed from the product rather than kept beside it. The room-clear drop and the shop stock keep their current mechanics.
- DEC-004 [AMB:AMB-004] [CQ-004] [CA-004]: `HudFloorBanner` is `NonPerformance "a timed floor announcement no steady-state workload frame holds; covered by production raster and catalog evidence"`. `HudHearts`, `HudCurrency`, `HudActiveCharge` and `HudMinimap` are each `RequiredIn [ "firing"; "maximum-content" ]` with `MaximumExpected = 1`, inheriting the retired `ui.hud-score` driver's disposition. `HudScore` is removed from the inventory, the catalog and the driver set in one edit, so the three sets never disagree.
- DEC-005 [CQ-005] [CA-005]: Every workload's `Authored` digest is re-declared from the value the candidate's own `PerformanceEvidence` run emits, and the reason it moved is recorded in `evidence.yml` and in the cycle report: the reflective canonical encoding of the initial model changed because two model field types changed, the intent policy string changed because `MaximumExpectedScale` and one structural budget changed, and three workloads' fenced source text changed. No digest is copied without the run that produced it also being green on every budget and every exact cost-driver equality.
- DEC-006 [CQ-006] [CA-006]: Two counters. `Model.TotalFloorPickupCandidates` counts player-versus-floor-pickup overlap tests per fixed step and is bound to a new cost driver `simulation.floor-pickup-candidates`, `RequiredIn [ "maximum-content" ]`. `Model.TotalWallQueries` absorbs the player's new wall-slab casts under the same `6 *` accounting the obstacle sweep uses, and `collision.shot-wall-queries` raises its `MaximumExpected` to the value the run emits. Both are exact-equality drivers in `maximum-content`.
- DEC-007 [CQ-007] [CA-007]: The M13 frames live in `readiness/014-m13-room-transition-pickups-world-state/frames/`, written by `scripts/render-m13-frames.fsx`. A test asserts that directory's subdirectory set is exactly the authored M13 frame names and that each holds a PNG, mirroring the M11 gate, so a frame silently dropped from the harness reds the suite.
- DEC-008 [CQ-008] [CA-008]: `wallThickness`, `doorwayRect` and the wall-slab geometry move to `Model`; `Render.wallThickness` and `Render.doorwayRect` remain as re-exports so no existing test or caller changes. `Render.roomWallsScene` and the player sweep consume the one `Model.roomWallSlabs` value.
- DEC-009: The effective touch-set is the item's declared `Paths:` plus `src/Rogue3/PerformanceEvidence.fs`, `src/Rogue3/EvidenceCommands.fs`, `scripts/render-m13-frames.fsx` (new) and `scripts/audit-binding-exceptions.json`. The first two are forced by the product's own coverage gate and its `M5ObstacleDrops` fixtures; the third is a new file that can collide with nothing; the fourth is the repository's own remedy for the audit-binding tripwire this change trips. The extension was declared on `EHotwagner/rogue3#12` before implementation and is disjoint from the only other in-flight item, `EHotwagner/rogue3#28`.
- DEC-010: Shop-slot and reward placement is a deterministic first-fit over an authored candidate ring inside the room, rejecting any candidate that is outside the wall band, inside a doorway opening plus its apron, inside the trapdoor footprint, or within the combined radius of one of the room's obstacles. First-fit over a fixed candidate list keeps the result a pure function of the room, so a frame and a test see the same placement, and adding a candidate cannot move an existing room's stock unless the earlier candidates stopped fitting.

## Accepted Deferrals
- DEC-011: Drawing the departed room's obstacles, drop, reward and shop stock through the slide is deferred (DEC-001). It needs the departed room's *room-local model state* to survive the crossing, which means either carrying a room snapshot on the transition or restoring room-local state from the floor graph — both are model-shape changes with their own determinism and performance consequences, and neither is needed for a crossing to read as a crossing. Filed as its own follow-up rather than left implicit.
- DEC-012: Making the room-clear drop (`Entities.CombatRoom.Drop`) a positioned, walk-onto pickup is deferred (DEC-003). It requires changing a record in `src/Rogue3/Entities.fs`, which is outside this item's touch-set, and it is a different mechanic from an obstacle drop. Filed as its own follow-up.
