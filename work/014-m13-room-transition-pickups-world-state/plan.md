- PI-002 PERF-PLAN: Focused skills loaded for their performance sections: `fs-gg-scene` and `fs-gg-symbology` (scene construction cost and the render-and-look loop), `fs-gg-collision` (broad/narrow phase and swept casts), `fs-gg-game-core` (the fixed step), `fs-gg-testing` and `fs-gg-playtest`. - PI-003 PERF-PLAN: Route analysis, stated before implementation. (a) Scene cost: the departed shell adds one room shell — one filled rect, at most eight wall slabs, at most four door groups and an optional trapdoor group — and only while a transition is live; the HUD split adds four group wrappers; the four state visuals add at most one node group per live actor for the telegraph and three constant groups for the player. The observed `maximum-content` maximum is 608 nodes against a 4096 budget, so the headroom is roughly 6.7x and the change is bounded in the low tens. (b) Fixed-step cost: pickup collection is one circle test per live floor pickup per step, bounded by the five destructible obstacles a room carries; the wall collider adds `6 * <slabs>` casts per step to the player's sweep under the same accounting `model.Obstacles` already uses. (c) Nothing is added to the shot, homing, AI or bullet paths.
- PI-004 PERF-SMOKE: The pre-implementation baseline is the committed `readiness/performance-evidence.json` at `6b4ed07` together with a green Release build and a 208-test Release suite, captured before any source change: `maximum-content` scene nodes 608, `secret-reveal` 695, `floor-generation` 222; `collision.shot-wall-queries` 736, `simulation.door-sensor-candidates` 8, `collision.combat-candidates` 2520. - PI-005 PERF-IMPLEMENT: Two deterministic counters make the new cost observable rather than argued: `Model.TotalFloorPickupCandidates` bound to a new `simulation.floor-pickup-candidates` driver, and the existing `Model.TotalWallQueries` absorbing the player's wall-slab casts under `collision.shot-wall-queries`.
